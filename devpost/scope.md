# ScopeSprint

ScopeSprint turns a beginner's small web app idea and available time into a manageable plan, then helps them track progress as they complete it.

## The Unique Kernel
It makes an overwhelming idea feel achievable by breaking it into prioritized, smaller tasks with clear completion goals. When an idea is too large, ScopeSprint should suggest a smaller MVP instead of planning a production-scale application.

## Who It's For
Students and first-time builders working on small, beginner-friendly web app projects. They want to move from a big idea to achievable steps without feeling overwhelmed. Their current alternative was not discussed.

## The Core Loop
A beginner enters a project idea and time budget, gets a prioritized plan of smaller tasks with clear completion goals, completes and edits tasks as they work, and checks progress that remains saved when they return.

## Inspiration & Identity
No specific design inspiration or visual direction has been shared yet.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
The learner is building ScopeSprint for the Devpost Build With AI: Basics hackathon and wants to learn how to use an AI coding agent to plan, build, test, and debug a working web app with confidence.

## What "Working" Looks Like
In a short screen demo, a beginner enters a small web app idea and available time, sees a prioritized plan with clear completion goals, completes a few tasks, and sees the progress update and remain saved after a page refresh. For an oversized idea, the plan should suggest a smaller MVP rather than a production-scale build.

## The POC Boundary
Build a simple, responsive browser-based proof of concept focused on small, beginner-friendly web app ideas. Include idea and time input, a prioritized MVP plan with clear completion goals, task editing and completion, progress tracking, browser persistence, and a useful sample project. Keep the experience honest about how plans are produced: do not claim a real AI model unless one is genuinely integrated. Avoid a backend or paid API unless the technical plan shows it is essential.

## Later
Broader project types beyond small, beginner-friendly web apps are outside the first-version focus and can be reconsidered later.

## Explicitly Cut
- Production-scale plans for complex social networks or Uber-like clones; suggest a smaller MVP instead, because the first version is for beginner-friendly projects.
- Complex backend systems and payment processing, because they are outside the smallest useful proof of concept.
